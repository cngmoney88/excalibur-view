A steel model in 3D, measuring in it, and a Batch window that stays put.

A STEEL MODEL IN 3D

File -> Open Model (IFC)... now opens the model itself. Every part is on
screen in a moment, and the holes and copes are cut in while you look
around. Drag to turn it, right-drag to move it, scroll to zoom to the
pointer, or pick Iso, Top, Front, Back, Left or Right. Parallel takes the
perspective out for elevations and plans, and Turn spins it slowly for a
screen at a show.

Colour it by kind, profile, assembly, or by where each part's weight came
from, which shows any part the model didn't weigh in red. Hide a kind, cut
away everything above a height to look inside, and click any part for its
profile, mark, assembly, length and weight. Ctrl-click adds to the
selection, double-click goes to a part, Esc clears it.

The tonnage and the model work together: the 3D button on a row of By
profile or Every part lights those pieces up in the model.

MEASURING

Start measuring, then click two points. A point near a corner or an edge
catches on it. You get the distance, how far it runs across and up, and
each point's elevation, in feet and inches.

PICTURES

Save picture... writes the view as a PNG at twice its size on screen. With
the Transparent background it drops onto a web page or a slide as it is.

OPENING IFC FILES

On Windows, .ifc files get "Open in Excalibur View" on their right-click
menu and in Open with. Whatever opens them now stays the default. On the
Mac, Excalibur View shows under Open With for an IFC file.

FIXES

The Batch window, Combine included, no longer grows until it reaches both
edges of the screen.
