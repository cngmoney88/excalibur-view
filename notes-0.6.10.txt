Pick sheets in Thumbnails, then print just those or save them as a PDF.

PICKING SHEETS

In the Thumbnails panel, Ctrl-click (Cmd on a Mac) sheets to pick them,
Shift-click to pick a run, and Ctrl-Shift-click to add another run. A
plain click still goes to a sheet, and clears the picks. Picking doesn't
move the drawing, so you can keep looking at one sheet while you choose
others.

The line above the list says how many are picked, with Print, Save as PDF
and Clear beside it. Right-click a sheet for the same, or to pick every
sheet a search is showing.

PRINTING THEM

Print opens on the picked sheets. Ctrl+P does too.

SAVING THEM AS A PDF

Save as PDF writes the picked sheets, markups and all, into a new file
wherever you put it. The drawing you're in stays as it is and stays open.

THE DOCUMENT MENU

Rotate, Extract, Delete, Crop, Flatten and the other jobs that work on
chosen sheets open with the picked ones already filled in.
