across your company. You may not:

- offer it to others as a hosted or managed service,

**This is source-available, not open source.** The Elastic License 2.0 is not
an OSI-approved open-source licence, and calling it one would be misleading.
We would rather say what it is.

## Why

So a fabricator's compliance officer can read every line rather than take our
word, and so an IT department can build it themselves if their rules say they
must — without us handing a competitor the product.

## What is not here

- **The Office server** is commercial and closed.
- **The trade tool chests we sell** — steel, concrete and earthwork,
  electrical, mechanical, plumbing, general contractor — and the data behind
  them. The chest *format* is open and so is reading and writing one: build
  your own, for yourself or your company. The research is what is sold.
- **The signing keys.** Releases are signed with a key held offline. The
  public half is in `crates/hyperview/src/trust.rs`, which is how the program
  checks an update, and the private half has never been on a computer that
  faces a network.
