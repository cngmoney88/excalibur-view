TYPING ON THE SHEET

Pick Text Box and click where the words go, then type. The words go on the
sheet as you type them, in their own size, colour and weight, and the box
grows with them. Enter starts a new line. Drag out a box instead and the
words wrap inside it; the box gets taller as they run past the bottom.

Click anywhere else on the sheet, or press Esc, when you're done. You're
back to picking things up, so the box can be moved or sized straight away.
A box left with nothing typed in it goes away. Double-click the words to
change them.

The toolbar's font, size, colour, bold, italic and alignment change the
words while you're still typing them.

A callout is two clicks now, the thing it's about and where the words go,
and then you type. Its box grows away from what it points at, and the
leader stays on the middle of its side. The words of a Cloud+ grow the
same way. Typewriter words have nothing drawn round them, on the screen as
well as on paper.

FIXED

Words typed into a new text box could go nowhere. A quick click put the
box down and closed it in the same moment, so the typing landed on the
shortcuts instead. Clicking away to finish a box also put down another
empty one where you clicked.
